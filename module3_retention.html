🏆

Champions

Strategy: Protect & Expand

• DO NOT offer commodity discounts — they undermine perceived value.

• Use Module 2 basket analysis to push premium SKU combos (UHT Chocolate + IFCMP bundles).

• Assign dedicated, senior SRs. Never rotate these accounts without a handoff call.

• Invite to exclusive brand activations / first-look previews of new SKUs.

💎

Loyal Base

Strategy: Deepen AOV

• Push volume-based incentives: "Buy 10, get 1 free" on high-margin SKUs.

• Cross-sell into categories they haven't bought (use Module 2 basket analysis).

• Visit frequency should be at least bi-weekly. Any gap >14 days is a risk signal.

• Monthly performance reviews by territory manager for top 30% of Loyal Base.

🌱

Potential / Promising

Strategy: Build Purchase Habit

• Increase visit frequency — first 3 orders are the most critical. Habit is built by order #3.

• Recommend top 3 best-sellers from their territory. Don't overwhelm with full catalogue.

• Offer new-outlet onboarding incentive: credit note on 3rd successful order.

• Target: convert 30% to Loyal Base within 90 days.

⚠️

At-Risk

Strategy: Win-Back Campaign

• Manager-level (not SR-level) phone call within 48 hours of identification.

• Offer one-time re-activation incentive (not a discount — a free-trial of new SKU).

• If unresponsive after 2 attempts → escalate to Area Manager for in-person visit.

• Root cause: compare with Churn Risk Radar to see if they appear there too.

💀

Lost / Inactive

Strategy: Reactivation or Declassify

• Cross-reference with Drop-Off analysis: was it a Visit Gap or Service Gap?

• Visit Gap: deploy SR for cold reintroduction visit with fresh product samples.

• Service Gap: investigate pricing complaints / product fit before re-engaging.

• Any outlet inactive >180 days with no response → flag for territory review.

🚩

Red Flag Outlets

Strategy: SR Accountability Audit

• Pull the Red Flag CSV → assign each outlet to their responsible SR by territory.

• Demand written explanation for each outlet with >10 visits and 0 orders.

• If product mismatch: remove from SR's active list, re-evaluate territory assignment.

• If SR capability: place under Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with 30-day target.
