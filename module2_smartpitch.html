How to read the Monthly Trend Charts:
• Time Series: The main line chart shows your raw total revenue month by month. Look for overall upward or downward patterns.
• MoM % (Month-over-Month): The bar chart shows how much revenue grew (Green) or shrank (Red) compared to the previous month. It helps spot sudden performance drops.
• 3-Month Rolling Avg: A smoothed version of your revenue line. It averages the last 3 months to cancel out temporary one-month spikes and show the "true" direction of your business.
Monthly Revenue Trend

Monthly Revenue Performance TIME SERIES

Meaning: Tracks overall business growth. How it's calculated: Sum of all non-cancelled order amounts aggregated by month.

Month-over-Month Growth Rate MoM %

Meaning: Shows growth momentum. How it's calculated: ((Current Month Revenue - Previous Month Revenue) / Previous Month Revenue) × 100.

Revenue Momentum (3-Month Rolling Avg)

Meaning: Removes short-term spikes to reveal the true underlying trend. How it's calculated: The average revenue of the current month and the previous two months.
