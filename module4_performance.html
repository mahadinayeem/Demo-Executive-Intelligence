Quadrant Training Interventions Matrix
• Closing & Objection Handling Gap (High Calls, Low Strike): Rep visits enough shops but fails to close orders. Requires product pitch & objection handling training.
• Route Discipline & Work Rate Deficit (Low Calls, High Strike): Rep converts well when visiting, but visits too few shops. Needs PJP route planning & call discipline.
• Basket Size Underperformance (High Strike, Low AOV): Rep secures orders easily but ticket size is small. Needs multi-SKU and trade scheme upselling coaching.
• Full Pipeline Shadowing (Low Calls, Low Strike): Struggles across both activity and closing. Requires 14-day joint field shadowing with a senior mentor.
